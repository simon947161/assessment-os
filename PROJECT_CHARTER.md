# AssessmentOS Project Charter v0.1

## Project

AssessmentOS

## First product

WHS Living Book — Painting and Decorating Pilot

## Founder direction

Deliver a small, useful and testable WHS learning website quickly for Dan and his learners. The project must produce a working result before expanding into a larger education platform.

## Problem

Current WHS learning resources are comprehensive but heavily document-centred. Learners must move through long, linear pages, while teachers repeatedly explain the same safety decisions. The result is high cognitive load, low engagement and substantial teacher effort.

## Product hypothesis

A short, visual and scenario-based mission can help learners practise authentic WHS judgement more effectively than passive reading alone, while preserving the formal workbook and assessment process.

## MVP mission

**Mission 01 — Spot the Hazards: Painting Workshop**

A learner enters a simulated painting workshop and identifies ten hazards. For each hazard, the learner must:

1. identify what is wrong;
2. recognise the possible consequence;
3. judge the level of risk;
4. choose the most appropriate control;
5. decide whether work must stop and who should be informed.

## Learning loop

`SPOT → THINK → CONTROL → ACT → REPORT`

## Target users

- Primary: Painting and Decorating learners
- Trial teacher: Dan Grammarson
- Future: other construction trades and VET units

## Delivery constraints

- Fast delivery is more important than broad feature coverage.
- The pilot must work without login or installation.
- No student personal information is collected.
- The pilot does not replace an approved assessment tool.
- The pilot does not make formal competent/not-yet-competent decisions.
- TAFE NSW copyrighted text and images must not be copied into the public site.
- All learning content must be rewritten as original scenario-based material and mapped internally to source concepts.

## Definition of done — MVP v0.1

- Public working URL
- Ten hazards implemented
- Immediate feedback for correct and incorrect choices
- Progress and final result shown
- Retry supported
- Usable on a classroom computer and phone
- Basic accessibility and keyboard operation
- Dan trial guide included
- Content validation checklist completed

## Non-goals for v0.1

- Full CPCCWHS2001 coverage
- Student accounts
- Teacher administration dashboard
- AI marking
- Formal assessment replacement
- LMS integration
- Database or analytics service
